01 · What is a Brokerage

The bridge between
buyer, seller, and deal.

The model

Commission at closing

Between 3% and 6% of the property value. On a $3M condo, one deal is significant. Everything the business does exists to bring more deals to that moment, faster.

Blackbook's edge

Floor plans and building depth

Most brokerages show listings. Blackbook shows context: floor plans, building histories, pre-construction timelines. The client understands the property before they ever visit.

Three clients

Not the same conversation

Buyer, seller, investor. Same brokerage but three completely different journeys from the very first interaction.

The advisor matters as much as the listing

More inventory means clients have real options. The brokerage that stays present and informed throughout the entire decision wins.

03 · AI + Luxury

Not everything needs AI.
But the right things do.

Where it works

Speed without losing the feel

A lead at 11pm deserves an immediate, on-brand response. Not a generic auto-reply. AI handles the first moment so the agent picks it up in the morning with full context.

Where it works

One conversation per client type

A buyer and an investor are not the same person. AI routes and personalizes so each client gets a conversation that feels built specifically for them.

Where it works

Agents focus on closing

Listing descriptions, follow-ups, activity logging. AI handles the repeatable work. The agent handles the relationship and the negotiation.

Where to be careful

Luxury clients feel generic immediately

A templated response to a $5M buyer damages trust in seconds. Every AI touchpoint has to feel considered, or it becomes a liability.

04 · Marketing

Quality over quantity.
Always.

What works
What does not
Expert video content: market reports, building tours, neighborhood context.
Generic listing posts. Every brokerage does this.
CTAs that create curiosity. "Get the insider report" not "Contact us now."
Promotional language. Luxury clients buy status. Cheap messaging kills the brand.
Content that educates. The client who understands the market trusts the brokerage that taught them.

05 · How I'd Approach It

Four priorities.
In this order.

01

Connect content to the pipeline

487 videos, a blog, Instagram, TikTok. None of it connected to a CRM entry. A smart, on-brand CTA at each touchpoint that captures interest and routes it into the right follow-up sequence.

02

Qualify what is already in the CRM

Use what already exists in Follow Up Boss: how a lead came in, what they asked, whether they called or messaged. Classify each one as buyer, seller, or investor. Clean data first, then build on top of it.

03

Fast, on-brand response at any hour

A lead that waits has already called another brokerage. An intelligent first response, premium and specific, connected to Follow Up Boss so no lead is ever left without an answer.

04

Seller and investor journeys that match the brand

The buyer experience is strong. The seller goes to a separate page that breaks the premium feel. The investor has no path at all. Both need the same level of depth inside the Blackbook experience.
